Model assumptions

Detection, the sign of n₂ and absorption are independent selections. A common strength control scales the effective phase and selected absorptive response. Closed aperture uses fixed S = 0.25. Open aperture collects all transmitted power. Linear absorption is position independent. Saturable absorption uses α(I) = α₀/(1 + I/Isat), with fixed α₀L = 0.7 and focal I₀/Isat from 0 to 5. Transmittance is normalized to the low-intensity reference. Packets represent absolute transmitted power. The combined refractive/absorptive profile is a radial illustration without nonlinear diffraction propagation or temporal pulse integration.
